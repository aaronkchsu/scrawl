FOR VIEWERS

Your explorer. Your call.

Use your streamer’s Twitch chat. No extra app or sign-in.

  1. !join

    Get an explorer.

    Your name appears at the bottom of the stream.

  2. !dig

    Start a climb.

    You climb automatically when a spot opens. Sending it again won’t skip the queue.

  3. !keep / !risk

    Keep your loot, or go higher.

    At the stop: !keep saves it. !risk tries for more, but a fall can lose this climb’s loot. No reply for 45 seconds keeps it.

With a partner stream, Keep can also send a rescue rope to their camp. Your earlier loot and XP stay yours.

Gear & chat-made caves

!equip shield blocks one hit. !equip boots skips gaps. !equip lamp finds extra ore. You need to own the item first.

!idea bees adds an idea for the next AI cave. Your host chooses when to create it.

    Game settings OPTIONAL

    New climbs only. At most one rock and 1 HP per climb. Switching off cancels incoming rocks.

    Server details & limits

    Normal climbs are free. Creating a cave uses one paid AI request within the server’s request cap. Caves reset with the session or server; collected finds stay in the backpack. Demo and Twitch inventories are separate.

    Twitch accounts reconnect after a server restart. Hype Train and gift subs use EventSub; Bits, regular subscriptions, Channel Points, and raids are not connected yet.

    Two remote streamers need one shared HTTPS server. Set BASE_URL to that address and CONTROL_TOKEN to a long secret. Keep .runtime for saved progress. Never put credentials in OBS URLs.
